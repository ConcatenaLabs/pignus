Version 2 loans

Repay in part, add collateral in place, any loan size.

How version 2 works
A version 2 vault keeps its terms and its debt beside one Simplicity program and reads the collateral from the coin. Anyone may repay part or all of a loan and anyone may add collateral in place. A liquidation needs the oracle's price signed over its live beacon, newer than the last one the vault used, and only while the vault is under its threshold; it takes only what brings the vault back to its target and forces the rest back. A take, a repayment and a top-up are signed by your wallet's contract engine, which adds your coins, recomputes every script, runs the program and shows its own five points before it signs. A liquidation, a recovery and a refund are made by pignus-cli.

Check a repurchase

For shares and other assets whose issuer decides who may hold them.

How a repurchase works
A restricted (OpenDAMP) asset cannot sit in a lending vault: every output carrying it must pay the holder's own covenant address. What can be built is a repurchase: you sell the asset to the lender, and their obligation to sell it back is secured by a bond in a covenant here. Settling one is a single OpenDAMP transfer that also spends the bond vault; pignus-cli repo-settle composes it, and the lender signs it with their OpenDAMP key under the issuer's current policy. A lender who cannot produce that signature cannot return the asset, and the bond is all you keep if they cannot. This form is the half that protects you beforehand: it rebuilds the bond vault's address from the terms and checks it against the funded coin, its asset and its amount.

Design and security
checking this page's loan code… chain… book…

Every loan's address is rebuilt in your browser from the terms shown and compared with the chain before anything is signed, and this page refuses to build a transaction when the two disagree. It holds no keys: your wallet shows you its own reading of every transaction before you approve it. If the two readings differ, reject it and say so.

Fees are paid in whichever asset you choose when you confirm, from those your wallet holds and the network accepts; no asset is preferred. For a loan against bitcoin, the Bitcoin fee to take your collateral back is fixed when the loan is taken and shown before you fund it. pignus-cli does everything this page does from your own node; a bitcoin loan begun one way is finished the same way.

The whole design and its security analysis are in the design document.
