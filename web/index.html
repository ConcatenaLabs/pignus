Open offers

Each offer holds a lender's principal in a covenant, so you can take it while they are offline. Taking one locks your collateral in a vault whose address the offer's own script recomputes from your wallet — it cannot pay anyone else, and nobody can point it elsewhere. Repay any time before maturity and the collateral comes straight back.

Lend

Your principal goes into an offer covenant with the terms baked in. Borrowers take it while you are offline; you cannot oversell it and cannot change a term afterwards. Anything untaken comes back to you after the expiry, permissionlessly, to the address this wallet already has.

Loans

Health is the current price over the liquidation price. Under 1.00 anyone may liquidate, and the covenant forces the surplus back to the borrower whoever does it — a liquidation is a race for the bonus, not a seizure. After maturity a loan can be called at any price; long after, the lender can sweep it without an oracle.

Check a repurchase regulated assets — not a loan

A regulated (OpenDAMP) asset cannot sit in a lending vault: every output carrying it must pay the holder's own covenant address, so on default nobody could ever reach the collateral. What can be built is a repurchase: you sell the asset to the lender, and their obligation to sell it back is secured by a bond in a covenant here. Settling one needs the asset's own machinery, so the transaction is composed elsewhere; this page does the half that protects you — rebuild the bond vault's address from the terms and check it against the funded coin.

What this page does, and what it cannot do

Every vault address here is derived in your browser from the terms shown and compared with what is actually on chain before anything is signed. That check is the whole of the non-custodial claim, and it is why this page refuses to build a transaction when an offer's address does not match its terms, rather than letting your wallet ask you about it.

It cannot move your money: it holds no keys, and your wallet shows you its own reading of every transaction before you approve it. If the two disagree, reject it — and tell us.

Network fees are paid in whichever asset your wallet holds; Sequentia has no privileged fee coin. Prefer the command line, or your own node? pignus-cli does everything this page does, from a node wallet.
